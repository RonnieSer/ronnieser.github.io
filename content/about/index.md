I'm a master's student in geology at the University of Texas at El Paso, working on ways to use machine learning to help map the Moon's south pole. I bring together satellite observations, terrain data, and geological interpretation to understand the landscapes we see from afar.

A lot of my work happens between maps and code. I'm building my skills in Python and ArcGIS, and exploring how these tools can help us recognize patterns in the terrain. What I find most interesting is what those patterns can tell us about a planet's past.

Beyond my current research, I'm curious about planetary tectonics and how activity deep inside a world shapes its surface. From subduction on Earth to the icy world of Europa, I'd love to understand how these connections work under different conditions. Learning to model those processes is a direction I'm excited to explore.

Thanks for stopping by! Have a look around for my research and a few stories from the field.
